WHO WE ARE

Service. Compassion. Community.

Iron Horse Charities Inc. exists to meet needs that often go unmet in our communities. Through volunteer-led fundraising and partnerships, we provide assistance to veterans, children, and families while strengthening community engagement.

Our work is built around integrity, service, respect, stewardship, and community.

Our Mission

To improve the lives of veterans, children, and members of the community through charitable programs, volunteerism, and financial assistance.

Our Vision

To become a trusted Missouri charity recognized for strengthening communities through compassion, service, and partnerships.
